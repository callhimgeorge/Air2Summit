# Assembly Notes

These notes apply only to open components in this repository.

- Check fit before use.
- Check threaded connections for proper engagement and retention.
- Inspect parts for wear, cracking, loosening, or other damage before field use.
- Do not force mismatched parts or interfaces.
- Do not use uncertified parts in safety-critical situations.

Final production hardware may differ from these files depending on supplier selection, manufacturing constraints, and prototype validation.
