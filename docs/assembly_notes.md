# Assembly Notes

These notes apply to the open components currently intended for public release, including tips, mounts, and accessories.

## General Notes

- Verify thread fit, engagement length, and shoulder seating before final assembly.
- Check printed or machined parts for cracks, poor layer adhesion, burrs, and dimensional drift.
- Avoid forcing mismatched interfaces. Rework the part or update the model if fit is not correct.
- Use threadlocking compound only where it does not interfere with serviceability or plastic compatibility.
- Confirm that accessory mounts do not interfere with hand position, folding geometry, or ground clearance.

## Tips and Wear Parts

- Treat tip components as consumable wear items.
- Match material and tread or contact geometry to the expected terrain.
- Inspect tips regularly for loosening, splitting, embedded debris, and abnormal wear.
- Replace parts that show impact damage or reduced retention.

## Mounts and Accessories

- Keep accessory mass low and close to the pole axis where possible.
- Do not use open accessory mounts as structural rescue, climbing, or fall-arrest hardware.
- Validate retention under vibration, repeated impacts, moisture exposure, and temperature cycling if the part will be used outdoors.

## Safety Notice

Open components in this repository are not certified safety equipment. Unless a part is explicitly documented as tested and approved for a defined load case, assume it is experimental.

Any non-certified part should be bench-tested before field use and monitored during service. If failure could lead to injury, loss of balance, or equipment damage, do not rely on an unvalidated component.
